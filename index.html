About the project

One brand, two directions

Design approach

Heritage emphasizes strong contrast and a clear service hierarchy. Photo Series brings business imagery into the same yellow-and-black visual system. Each direction adapts to multiple formats while keeping the message recognizable.

My role

Brooke Howard / BaBBled LLC: graphic design, creative direction, marketing messaging, layout development and mockup presentation. This gallery presents concepts and review previews, without claiming final printing, production approval or measured campaign results.

Use the side controls to explore each piece, or enlarge an image for detail. Public business contact details remain part of the brochure artwork. Client billing and correspondence are excluded from this portfolio presentation.
